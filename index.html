Scientific Asset Manager (SAM)

This unique icon set was meticulously crafted for an application designed to organize assets and collect scientific data. Each icon was created from scratch, tailored specifically to meet the needs of scientists operating in the field. These icons are one-of-a-kind, ensuring unparalleled functionality and intuitive usability in various scientific contexts. The distinctive designs facilitate efficient data management and enhance the overall user experience for researchers and field scientists.

SeaGlide by RoboNation

download

As the lead designer in an exciting project, I was tasked with creating a comprehensive manual for a STEM DIY kit for kids called SeaGlide by RoboNation. The manual, which is over 100 pages long, features colorful illustrations, beautiful photography, and all the necessary steps to complete the build of the first autonomous underwater vessel. Working on this project is an amazing experience that allowed me to combine my passion for design and STEM education, and I am thrilled to have been a part of creating an educational tool that inspires and engages children in the field of robotics and underwater exploration.
