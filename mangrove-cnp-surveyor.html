Mobile app design

Mangrove CNP Surveyor

planetwerx.org/mangrove ↗

UI design for a citizen-science mobile app built to track Mangrove CNP, a fungal disease responsible for mangrove die-offs across Florida and other coastal regions worldwide. The app walks volunteers through a simple three-step survey: photograph the leaf, photograph the branch, log the observed symptoms — turning an ordinary walk near the mangroves into usable data for researchers tracking the disease's spread.
